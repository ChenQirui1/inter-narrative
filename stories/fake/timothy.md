# Timothy

**Background:**  
Timothy is an introverted, obedient person who does not like being the centre of attention. Often fearful of falling short, he rarely shows proactiveness and is scared of making errors, lest he make a mistake that implicates others. He often has difficulties carrying out easy tasks, and is prone to anxiety, and even hyperventilation. With his wide-eyed look, modest spectacles, and plain attire, he doesn’t stand out in a crowd. Having been diagnosed with anxiety since young, his schooling years were fraught with bullying, from both teachers and schoolmates, deeply scarring him. In spite of these challenges, Timothy remains silently resolute, always doing his utmost to not let others down.

**Motivation:**  
Fear of letting people down, dying
