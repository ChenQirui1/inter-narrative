# Zhuge

**Background:**  
Resurrected from the Shu-Han dynasty by GOR. He was a brilliant strategist. He is extremely intelligent and a very nonchalant guy. He is the chillest guy in the room. Also, he is great at manipulating. He was probably like the first gaslighter in history. He understands human psychology really well but doesn’t understand one irrational emotion- love. He is celibate and only cares about cats. By mindset, he is kinda conservative given the time period and the environment he grew up in, but he will be open to liberal ideas if it’s introduced to him. He was very loyal to the state in his life. He prioritizes the peace and stability of the state, and once he has a goal, he does anything to achieve it.

**Motivation:**  
He is most likely to be a socialist in the modern world. Tries to figure out what is GOR.
