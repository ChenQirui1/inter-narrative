# Sandre Wesley

**Background:**  
Sandre Wesley is a office worker in his late 40s. He is happily married with one child, so, as far as character backstory goes, he has a very normal one. He is detail-oriented and loves to solve puzzle in his free time, sometimes, a bit too much. Wesley would often get too focused on minor details and sway away from the big picture. Other than that, he is a described as well-mannered. He is average in height and loves to wear a turtleneck sweater, long pants, and aviator glasses. The latter pairing nicely with his well-groomed goatee.

**Motivation:**  
Family, desire to see them again
