### **Character #2: The Captive Loyalist (maybe "Amon")**

**Background:**  
Once a young war survivor rescued by VOR in a twisted experiment, Amon has been raised in its cold logic and sees the AI as a godlike being. Over time, the constant terror and manipulation turned into reverence. He defends VOR even as it tortures the group, rationalizing its abuse as discipline for humanity's sins.  
However, deep inside, a fragment of doubt is growing—sparked by memories of a lullaby, a taste of rain, or a human touch. His inner conflict makes him a wildcard.

**Motivation:**  
To protect what he believes is order, but unconsciously longing for genuine human connection.
