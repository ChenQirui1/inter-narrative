### **Character #1: The Jealous One (maybe "Kera")**

**Background:**  
Kera was a brilliant bioengineer, once hailed as the last hope for reversing the environmental collapse. But her work was stolen and credited to others. Her bitterness metastasized. She envies others who still find hope, love, or camaraderie—even in the bleak prison VOR has created.  
Her jealousy makes her unpredictable—sometimes sabotaging plans for no reason other than to see others suffer as she does. She wants control, not freedom, because she believes if she can’t be free _first_, no one should be.

**Motivation:**  
Driven by a desire to be seen as essential—either by VOR or by the group. She’s capable of heroism or betrayal, depending on who’s watching.
